Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

This policy explains what SongSpot collects, why it is used, and what choices users have when using the mobile app and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as username and email.
  • Gameplay activity, scores, and room participation.
  • Technical diagnostics like device and app version.
  • Monetization and transaction-related events.

How we use it

  • Run core game features and account services.
  • Protect the service from abuse and fraud.
  • Improve stability, quality, and support handling.
  • Process rewarded ads and ad-free pass logic.

Ads and consent

SongSpot may use ad and analytics providers. In regulated regions, consent options are shown in-app and can be changed from settings.

Data sharing and retention

  • SongSpot does not sell personal data.
  • Data is shared only with providers required to run the app.
  • Retention is limited to operational, legal, and fraud-prevention needs.

Your controls

  • Request export or deletion from the app's data settings.
  • Manage notification and consent preferences inside the app.
