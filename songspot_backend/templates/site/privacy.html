What SongSpot collects, why it is used, and what you can do about it. Plain list, no surprises buried in the middle.

Last updated · March 2, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: username and email.
  • Gameplay activity: scores, streaks, sessions and duels.
  • Technical diagnostics: device model and app version.
  • Purchase events, when you buy something.

What it is used for

  • Running the game and your account.
  • Protecting the service from abuse and fraud.
  • Fixing crashes and improving stability.
  • Processing rewarded ads and the ad-free pass.

Ads, analytics and consent

SongSpot may use advertising and analytics providers. Analytics is opt-in: it stays off until you turn it on. In regulated regions the consent options are shown when you first open the app, and every choice can be changed later from Settings.

Sharing and retention

  • SongSpot does not sell personal data.
  • Data is shared only with the providers required to run the app.
  • Retention is limited to operational, legal and fraud-prevention needs.
Your controls

You can take
your data back

Export

Settings → My Data → Export data. You get a JSON file of everything tied to your account.

Deletion

Settings → My Data → Delete account. It is permanent, and it removes the associated data.
